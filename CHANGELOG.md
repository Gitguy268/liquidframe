- Local OCR, pet/object suggestions, sampled video search, Natural Earth offline places, and curation.
- Video processing/proxies, Live Photo stacks/effects, expanded new-copy exports, slideshow projects/playback/video, and PDF printing.
- Optional Swift PhotoKit/depth bridge; capability gates and network retrieval disabled.
- Regression checks for migration, privacy, history, source revisions, video/audio, OCR, slideshow/print, and large-catalog browsing.
